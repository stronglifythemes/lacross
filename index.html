{ Studio }

Lacross is a two-person picture studio in Porto. We look for the small part of an image that carries the whole thing, lift it out, and build the page, the book or the wall around what is left.

Most pictures are asked to say too much. We work with photographers, publishers and a few patient brands to decide what a picture is actually about, and then we get everything else out of its way.

{ Practice }

Six things we do, filed under the six pieces we took.

  1. Picture editing

    No. 1

    From four thousand frames to the forty that hold. We edit on paper, on a table, slowly.

  2. Cropping and reframing

    No. 2

    The same negative, read again. New formats for covers, posters and screens without losing the picture.

  3. Sequencing

    No. 3

    Order, pairs and pauses for books, portfolios and exhibitions. Which picture follows which, and why.

  4. Art direction

    No. 4

    Briefs, shot lists and a second pair of eyes on set, for campaigns that should look like nobody tried.

  5. Print production

    No. 5

    Proofing, paper and press checks with two printers we trust, in editions from one to three thousand.

  6. Archive care

    No. 6

    Scanning, naming and keeping. We put old boxes in an order someone else can find their way through.
